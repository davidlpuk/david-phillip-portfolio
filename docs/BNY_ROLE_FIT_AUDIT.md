# BNY Director / Platform Design Lead Portfolio Fit Audit

## Overall Fit

David's background is a strong match for the BNY profile. The strongest evidence is his financial-services depth, design-system leadership, regulated delivery experience, team scaling and ability to work across Product, Engineering, Compliance, Risk and executive stakeholders.

The portfolio needed clearer signalling for the specific language in this role. The previous version positioned David as a senior UX/product design leader, but did not make "platform design", "standards", "research consultation", "usability evaluation" and "design governance" explicit enough.

## Strong Matches

- Financial services experience across Coutts, HSBC, BlackRock, Barclays, Deutsche Bank, Schroders and TSB.
- Design-system and standards work, including Figma migration, reusable patterns and governance.
- Research-led product practice: usability testing, interviews, workshops, heuristic evaluation and evidence-led decisions.
- Cross-functional influence with Product, Engineering, Compliance, Risk, Legal and executive stakeholders.
- Team leadership: scaling teams, mentoring designers, creating rituals and raising design maturity.
- AI relevance: practical AI-assisted research, critique and prototyping, framed with judgement and governance.

## Gaps To Watch

- If applying directly to BNY, add one sentence in the CV and cover letter about platform design standards and reusable UI guidelines.
- Keep claims about metrics evidence-based. Where source evidence is unavailable, phrase them as portfolio material or discuss in private walkthroughs.
- The portfolio should eventually include a visible example of design-system artefacts, guidelines or governance model if confidentiality allows.
- Server-side case-study protection is required before using private material in a live application funnel.
